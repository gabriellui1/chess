Welcome to the chess menu!
Choose one of the commands:

game <white-player> <black-player>
    starts a new game with <white-player> and <black-player>
    <white-player>: human | computer1 | computer2 | computer3 | computer4
        choose the type of player playing with the white pieces
    <black-player>: human | computer1 | computer2 | computer3 | computer4
        choose the type of player playing with the black pieces

setup
    enters setup mode to set up your own initial board configurations

help
    list of commands in your current state

quit
    exits chess
