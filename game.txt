List of available commands while playing a game:

move
    on a computer player, calculates and performs the next move  

move <source> <destination>
    on a human player, performs a move on one of your pieces. moves your piece from the square <source> to the square <destination>

    <source>: [a1-h8]
        source square on the 8x8 board. letter [a-h] specifies file and number [1-8] specifies rank

    <destination>: [a1-h8]
        destination square on the 8x8 board. letter [a-h] specifies file and number [1-8] specifies rank

    ex. "move e2 e4"

move <source> <destination> <promoting-piece>
    on a human player, performs a promoting pawn move on your pawn. moves your pawn from the square <source> to the square <destination> and promotes to the piece type <promoting-piece>

    <promoting-piece>: Q | R | B | N
        the piece that the pawn promotes to, shortened to their starting letter

    ex. "move a7 b8 B"

undo
    reverts the last move, allowing a player to try a different line

resign
    forfeits the game as the current player and hands the win over to the opponent

help
    list of commands in your current state

quit
    exits chess
